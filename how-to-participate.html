Giveaway Draw — How to Participate

Watch the short video below to see exactly how the giveaway works, from signing up to the weekly draw.

Giveaway Rules
  1. Entry is free. No purchase necessary.
  2. Sign up once on the Sign Up page with your name, email and YouTube handle. You'll be contacted by email if you win, so make sure your details are correct.
  3. Each week, a new video starts a 15-day countdown, shown on the Quiz page.
  4. To enter that week's draw: watch the featured video in full, comment the word "DONE" under it on YouTube, then submit your real answer to the quiz question on the Quiz page. Both steps are required — the YouTube comment alone does not count as an entry.
  5. You must be signed up before you can submit an answer. Already signed up on a different device? Use the Login page.
  6. One entry per person, per video. Submitting multiple answers for the same video does not increase your chances — only your latest answer counts. Entries from duplicate or fraudulent accounts will be disqualified.
  7. Participants must be 18 or older. Employees, family members, or close associates of Walking in London are not eligible to enter.
  8. When the countdown ends, the video's final view count is locked in and the giveaway moves to "Draw within 24h."
  9. The winner is picked at random among eligible entries — those who commented DONE on YouTube and submitted the correct answer on the Quiz page — and announced within 24 hours of the countdown ending.
  10. Prize amount is determined by the video's final view count — see the prize tier table on the Home page.
  11. Winners are contacted via the email provided at sign-up. If a winner cannot be verified or does not respond within 5 days, a new winner will be drawn from the remaining eligible entries.
  12. Prizes are paid via PayPal or bank transfer, depending on what the winner can provide.
  13. Open worldwide, except where prohibited by local law.
  14. Winners' YouTube handles may be featured on the Quiz page, with their consent.
  15. This promotion is in no way sponsored, endorsed, administered by, or associated with YouTube.
  16. Walking in London reserves the right to update these rules at any time; the current version always applies.
