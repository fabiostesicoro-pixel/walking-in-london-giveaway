Giveaway Draw — How to Participate

Watch the short video below to see exactly how the giveaway works, from signing up to the weekly draw.

Giveaway Rules
  1. Entry is free. No purchase necessary.
  2. Sign up once on the Sign Up page with your name, email and YouTube handle. You'll be contacted by email if you win, so make sure your details are correct.
  3. Each week, a new video starts a 15-day countdown, shown on the Draw page.
  4. To enter that week's draw, answer the quiz question featured in the video, by commenting your answer under that video.
  5. One entry per person, per video. Multiple comment entries on the same video will not increase your chances. Entries from duplicate or fraudulent accounts will be disqualified.
  6. Participants must be 18 or older. Employees, family members, or close associates of Walking in London are not eligible to enter.
  7. When the countdown ends, the video's final view count is locked in and the giveaway moves to "Draw within 24h."
  8. The winner is picked at random among eligible entries and announced within 24 hours of the countdown ending.
  9. Prize amount is determined by the video's final view count — see the prize tier table on the Prizes page.
  10. Winners are contacted via the email provided at sign-up. If a winner cannot be verified or does not respond within 5 days, a new winner will be drawn from the remaining eligible entries.
  11. Prizes are paid via PayPal or bank transfer, depending on what the winner can provide.
  12. Open worldwide, except where prohibited by local law.
  13. Winners' YouTube handles may be featured on the Draw page, with their consent.
  14. This promotion is in no way sponsored, endorsed, administered by, or associated with YouTube.
  15. Walking in London reserves the right to update these rules at any time; the current version always applies.
